# Marquee, dark first

Of five directions explored, "Marquee" was chosen: Anton over Archivo, an ink
ground, marquee red and bulb amber, photographs bled to the edges with the
headline set inside the image. It ships dark-only; a light theme comes later.

The vocabulary is drawn from what the organisation already produces — concert
flyers and a small-town marquee — and the near-black ground exists so that the
photography carries all of the colour. Two rejected alternatives are worth
remembering. A light variant of the same palette failed because the accent roles
were kept while the ground was swapped: amber cannot be text on cream, and the
bulbs and stage-lit photography have nothing to be lit against. A hybrid that
added hand-lettering, tape and rotation was rejected because per-instance art
direction degrades as soon as a non-designer adds a page.

## Consequences

- The palette is defined by **role** (accent-as-text vs accent-as-fill), not by
  colour name, so the deferred light theme is a palette assignment rather than a
  redesign.
- **Unresolved:** 27 long-form essays are the largest body of content on the site,
  and a near-black ground is tiring at 2,000 words. Flipping the reading sections
  to a light band inside the dark page worked in exploration. This must be settled
  before the essay block is built, not after.
